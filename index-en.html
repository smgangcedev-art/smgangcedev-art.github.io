One place, less to remember

Keep the things that matter
in one place.

01

Notes and idea inbox

Capture ideas with a shortcut and organize them with notes, tags and hashtags. Turn them into tasks or pin a separate note window. Use Save to confirm changes to the title and details.

02

Tasks and calendars

View all tasks or focus on today, this week or this month. Drag events in monthly and weekly calendars and resize either end to change their duration. The monthly calendar keeps a six-week layout and groups overflow under Show more. The weekly calendar shows 24 hours; press the weekly button to jump to the current time.

03

Anniversaries and countdowns

Record solar and lunar calendar anniversaries and see countdowns. Keep important dates together on your calendar.

04

Work progress

Record progress by date from the start of a task. Previous records continue across the calendar, and the date you reach 100% becomes the completion date.

05

Class timetables

Enter the semester dates and weekly classes to create recurring events. Student and instructor modes also manage canceled and make-up classes.

06

Focus and backups

Manage focus time with the large Pomodoro timer inside a task window. Keep your records with automatic local backups and file restore.

07

Weekly and monthly repeats

Repeat on selected weekdays, specific monthly dates or the last day of each month. Choose how to handle months without a 29th, 30th or 31st. Future events appear on the calendar, while visibility in task lists and remaining-task counts can be configured separately.

08

Quick entry in sentences

Enter Korean phrases such as “다음주목요일까지 보고서” (report by next Thursday) or “운동 매달 20일 시작 오늘 종료 26.12.31 20:00” (exercise every month on the 20th, starting today, ending December 31, 2026 at 20:00). The app recognizes dates, times, durations and hashtags. See examples in the small (?) help panel.

09

Make it fit your screen

The app remembers the position and size of the main bar, main window, calendar and notes. Adjust transparency, always-on-top and desktop modes, fonts, sizes, themes and button shapes, plus tab order and tag colors. Tag order in the input window and calendar follows the main window.

10

Completed records and reminders

Manage records with the completed archive, Undo and Trash. Set reminder timing for each event and choose up to three tasks to focus on today.

Allset · Optional Google connection

Back up to your Google Drive,
and connect your records across devices.

Allset integrated test versions that support Google connections provide backup, restore and manual sync using a Google account you choose. Connect the same Google account in compatible Windows, Mac and Android versions. Supported features vary by platform and version.

Version information: The free Microsoft Store edition provides local features only, without Google connections. The description below applies to test versions with Google support. Basic notes and scheduling do not require a Google connection.

Why are Google permissions needed?

Email and email verification information identify and display the connected account. To read and write Allset backups and sync records in Google Drive's app-specific storage, the app requests the drive.appdata permission.

What is transferred?

Backups may include notes, tasks, schedules, tags, completed records, goals and settings. Manual sync exchanges changes to supported items and device-specific change histories. Permission to read all ordinary Drive files is not requested.

When can I connect or disconnect?

Pressing Sync merges changes from both sides and allows conflicts to be reviewed or resolved. Automatic backup runs only when enabled by the user in a supported version. You can disconnect in the app and revoke access in your Google account.

Backup restore and manual sync are different features. Not all settings, window positions or project boards sync identically across devices. Google data is used to provide requested features, not for sale, advertising targeting or general-purpose AI model training.
