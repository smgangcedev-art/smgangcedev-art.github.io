Allset · Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

codepretender explains how Allset and this website process data. The free Microsoft Store edition 1.0.2 provides local features. The Google connection, account and team sharing descriptions below apply only to integrated test versions and other versions with those features. Versions without those features do not collect that information.

1. Records and backups on your device

Notes, tasks, schedules, recurring events, anniversaries and dates of birth, tags and hashtags, timetables, progress and completed records, goals, projects and settings are stored on your device by default. Basic local features require no registration.

Backups may include the records and settings covered by that version, creation times, operating system and device names. Local backups and exports are saved where you choose. Copies may remain externally if that location is external storage or a synced folder.

2. Optional Google connections and data use

  • Account information: Drive connections use email and email verification information to identify the account and show connection status. You authorize the connection on Google's login screen. The app does not receive or store Google passwords.
  • Permission scope: The Windows Drive connection requests openid, email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata . Drive access is for reading and writing Allset's app-specific storage, not for accessing all ordinary files in your Drive.
  • Backup and restore: Backups you request are stored in the connected Google account's app-specific storage. Selected backups are downloaded for restoration. Automatic backup runs when you enable it in a supported version.
  • Manual sync: When you run sync, changes to supported items are exchanged and merged between compatible Windows, Mac and Android versions. Sync data may include record IDs, change times, device IDs, change order and history. Conflicts and device-specific histories may be retained for review, resolution and prevention of lost changes. Real-time collaboration or identical syncing of all settings and project boards is not guaranteed.
  • Storage and protection: Google backup and sync data is stored in the connected Drive's app-specific storage and is not visible in the ordinary file list. Authentication tokens maintain the connection. On Windows, they are encrypted and stored on the device using operating system security features. If those features are unavailable, connection information is not saved.

3. Purposes and restrictions for Google data

Information received from Google is used for account connections and the backup, restore and manual sync features you request. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising targeting or general-purpose AI model training. Use of information received from Google APIs and transfer to other apps complies, including Limited Use requirements, with the Google API Services User Data Policy .

4. Optional accounts, purchases and team sharing

In versions with these features, signing in to Allset with Google uses Supabase authentication to process account identifiers, email and other login information for account identification and entitlement checks. This login is separate from personal Drive connections and does not itself grant access to all Drive files. If purchases are used, the payment service handles payment information; the app server processes order and account identifiers, payment status and other information needed to verify entitlements.

If you choose team sharing, designated shared schedules, team information, names, job titles, company names and author information are stored in Supabase and made available to authorized team members. Personal Drive backups are not automatically shared with teammates. Follow the relevant version's policies for server cleanup and local retention of team records. Copies already downloaded to devices may remain after server cleanup.

5. External services and this website

Personal Drive backups and sync records are sent to Google. Updates to holiday and lunar calendar data may request public files from GitHub; these requests do not include note or schedule contents. The website uses GitHub Pages and has no separate advertising tracking, visitor analytics scripts or inquiry form. Service providers may process connection information to handle requests and maintain security. Data using overseas services may be processed outside your country.

Google Privacy Policy · GitHub Privacy Statement · Supabase Privacy Policy

6. Retention, deletion and disconnection

  • Local records and backups are kept on your devices and at your chosen storage locations. Deleted records may remain as copies in Trash, backups or sync history. App Trash is cleared after up to 30 days and can also be permanently emptied.
  • Disconnecting Google removes connection information from the device and attempts to revoke Google access. If revocation is incomplete due to a network error or another issue, you can also use Google account connections to revoke access.
  • Disconnecting does not automatically delete existing Drive backup or sync files. You can delete Allset's hidden app data under Manage apps in Drive settings. If local data remains on a device that syncs again, it may be uploaded again later.
  • To delete everything, check each device's local data, local backup and export files, and Google app data separately. Uninstalling the app alone does not remove every copy.
  • The developer has no administration feature to directly view or delete personal device records or hidden app data in personal Drive accounts. For deletion of account or team sharing server data, contact the email address below.

7. Email inquiries and changes

When you email support, your email address, message and attachments are received and used to respond and resolve issues. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or unnecessary full backups. Request deletion of inquiry materials at the same email address.
