MailSet privacy

Privacy policy

Applies to: MailSet for Windows · Published October 24, 2026
Developer: codepretender · Contact: codepretender.dev@gmail.com

This information describes the behavior of the version prepared for release. MailSet prepares recipient lists and messages on your PC and sends them through the mail server you specify.

Information used by the app

The app uses names, reference or student IDs, emails, topics, billing information, message subjects and bodies, attachments and mail account settings that you import or enter. It processes them as needed to review recipients, personalize messages, send via SMTP, prevent duplicate sends and run schedules.

Local storage and protection

Mail settings and send history are stored on your PC. History may include recipient information, send times and status, and duplicate-check values. Schedules store copies of recipients, message bodies and attachments.

If you choose to remember the password, the app password is encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Scheduled jobs are protected in the same way. Not all general settings and send history are encrypted, so manage access to your Windows account and PC.

External transfers

Sending email transmits authentication information, recipients, message content and attachments to the selected provider's SMTP server. Data may also be retained by the provider and recipients' mail systems under their own policies. A specified Reply-To address is included in the message headers.

The app does not collect or transmit recipient lists, message bodies, attachments or app passwords to the developer's server, and it includes no advertising or usage analytics tracking. Information you send directly to support is retained in the email service and used to respond to your inquiry.

Retention and deletion

Local settings, send history and schedule data may remain until you delete them. Canceling a schedule stops sending; it does not delete its stored copies. To delete data, first cancel schedules, exit the app and check the data folder.

The data folder for the standard executable version is %LOCALAPPDATA%\AutoMailSendingApp . This folder name is retained for compatibility with earlier versions. The actual location may differ in the Store edition due to Windows app data isolation. Deleting data also removes saved settings, schedules and duplicate-send prevention records, so save any records you need first. Manage original Excel files, attachments and already-sent email separately.

Contact support to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information included in support emails. The developer cannot directly access local data on your PC or your mail account.

Changes

If processing changes, this page and its publication date will be updated.
