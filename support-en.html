Frequently asked questions

Are the 1.0.9 features in the current version?

Yes. Progress notes and selective export are included in version 1.2.2, currently available on Microsoft Store. Check the Store library for the latest update.

It includes progress-stage notes, selective text, CSV and clipboard export, project group selection and improved calendar dragging. It excludes Google login and sync, team sharing, paid purchases, ads and Excel export.

Are the features introduced in 1.0.6 still available?

Yes. The features added in 1.0.6 are included in the released version 1.2.2. Update from the Microsoft Store library.

It adds fixed titles and hashtags, a slim main bar, password setup popup, project end dates, an in-window Pomodoro timer and magnetic window alignment. Google sync, sharing, team features, Excel and ads are not included.

Can I use it on mobile?

The Android version is being prepared for public release. Download links will be announced on the official website when ready.

How much does PC–mobile sync cost?

Sync is listed at KRW 2,900 as a one-time purchase, not a subscription. In supported versions, connect the same Allset and Google accounts and press Sync on both devices to exchange records. The current free Store edition does not include sync.

Does the Store app include ads or paid sync?

The free Store edition has no ads, scheduled ad popups, Google sync, team sharing or Excel export. Registration and ad-removal purchases are not required. Mobile and cross-device sync are features of supported versions being prepared separately.

Where can I download it?

Download the free Windows edition from Microsoft Store .

Do I need to register or pay?

The free Windows edition requires no registration or payment. This website distinguishes it from mobile and sync features still in preparation. The 1.0.3 update adds projects, goals and multiple reminder times, excluding Google sync, team sharing, Excel export and ads. Version 1.0.3 has been released on Microsoft Store. Existing users can check for updates in the Store library.

Why are recurring events missing from my task list?

Check recurring-event and anniversary visibility under Settings → Display settings. They appear on the day by default. Calendar date cells and daily event lists also show future events.

How do I move my records to another computer?

Go to Settings → Local backup, choose Back up now and keep the file. Use Restore from file on the other computer. Back up its current records before restoring.

Where can I find input examples?

Press the small (?) button beside the title or input button. Examples for dates, recurring events, anniversaries and semester timetables are organized by topic.

Can I use it on Mac?

This free distribution is for Windows. Support for other operating systems will be announced when ready.
