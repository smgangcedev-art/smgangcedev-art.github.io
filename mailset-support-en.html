MailSet user guide

This guide was written for the version prepared for release. Features and screens may differ in the released version.

1. Choose a purpose and prepare recipients

Choose Education, Business, Personal work, Organization, Billing or Custom. Import an XLS, XLSX or CSV list and check names, emails, IDs and topics. Educational lists with student IDs and course information are also supported.

Add recipients not in the list and exclude those you do not want to contact. Email addresses can be edited before sending.

2. Check attachments

Include a reference ID, student ID or name in filenames to link files from an automatic matching folder, or select files for each recipient directly. Multiple files can be attached per person. Check individual results for duplicate names or records.

3. Configure your mail account and test

Enter the SMTP server, port, sender address and credentials. For Gmail, app passwords can be created after enabling 2-Step Verification, but may be unavailable under organization policies or account settings.

  • Google app password settings
  • Official Google app password help

To send through Gmail but receive replies at a school or work address, enter that address as Reply-To. The sender address will still be Gmail. Instructions for creating an app password are also available in the app's third tab.

Check the subject, body and preview, then use “Test to my email (1 message).” This sends an actual email, so check the account and attachments first.

4. Send to selected recipients or everyone

Select recipients with the checkboxes. Select one for a single send or several for a selected send. Use Select all or Deselect all to adjust the list; sending to everyone has a separate button. Success means acceptance by the SMTP server, not guaranteed inbox delivery or reading.

5. Scheduling and recurring billing

In the fourth tab, create a one-time or monthly schedule. It uses the PC's local time and requires the PC, app and internet connection to remain active. No mail is sent while the app is closed or the PC is asleep. Jobs over five minutes late are not automatically sent in a batch; check their status and run them manually.

If a scheduled date does not exist in a month, the last day of that month is used. Automatic billing dates align the billing month and due date with the send month. Amounts, recipients and attachments stay as registered; cancel and recreate the schedule to change them. Review stopped or failed jobs and prepare only the recipients who need another send.
