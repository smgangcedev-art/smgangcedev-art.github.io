Allset privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Local records

Tasks, notes, recurring events, anniversaries, dates of birth, tags, timetables, completed records and settings are stored on your device by default. Basic features work without login. File backups are saved to a location you choose.

Allset accounts and data sharing

If you choose Google login, your email, Google-provided account identifiers and email verification status are processed to authenticate your Allset account. Accounts use Supabase authentication and entitlement storage. Data sharing purchases are linked to your Allset user ID and can be checked across apps using that account. The app does not receive Google passwords. Android authentication tokens are kept in encrypted app-specific storage using Android Keystore.

Google Drive connection

When a data sharing user chooses to connect, app records and sync changes are transferred to Google Drive's app-specific storage (drive.appdata). Access to all ordinary Drive files is not requested. Changes are applied through manual sync; automatic checks only read and compare remote status. Allset login and Drive connections are separate. The Drive account can be checked on Google's authorization screen.

Web payments and entitlements

Payments are handled by Polar as Merchant of Record. The Android app only checks purchase entitlements and has no purchase feature or payment link. The app does not directly collect or store payment method information processed by the payment service. The server processes Allset user IDs, verified order IDs, products, payment and refund status, and verification times to grant access, apply refunds and prevent duplicates or misuse. Test orders do not grant production entitlements.

External services and security

Google and Supabase are used for authentication and entitlement checks; Polar handles web payments. These providers may process IP addresses and access logs while handling requests. The website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Network communication uses HTTPS. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising targeting or general-purpose AI training. The Limited Use requirements of the Google API Services User Data Policy are followed.

Deletion and retention

Request deletion of your account and linked data from the Allset account screen in app settings or the separate account deletion webpage. If you cannot sign in, contact support by email. Confirmation and processing details are sent to your registered email. Accounts, authentication tokens, entitlements and server connection information are subject to deletion. The purchase email, order number, product, payment status and payment date are kept for 5 years to verify transaction records, then deleted. Order information held by our payment provider Polar follows Polar's privacy policy. Device schedules, personal Drive files and exports are not automatically deleted; you can remove them yourself. Account deletion is separate from a payment refund.

Revoke Google permissions in your Google account connections. Delete Drive app-specific data separately under Manage apps in Drive settings. Local logout removes login information from the current device while preserving schedules.
