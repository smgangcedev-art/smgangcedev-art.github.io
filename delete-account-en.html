Request Allset account deletion

Sign in to the Allset account you want deleted. You can request deletion of account-linked information, including the account and purchase entitlements. We process requests within 10 days of the request date and send details to your registered email. To verify payments, the purchase email, order number, product, payment status and payment date are kept for 5 years, then deleted. Schedules, profiles and other account information are deleted.

Schedules and exported backups on your devices and files in your personal Google Drive are not automatically deleted. Remove them from each device and Drive if needed. Account deletion requests are separate from refund requests.
