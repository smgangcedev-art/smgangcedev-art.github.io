Refund Policy

Allset refund policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

1. Refunds for web payments

  • A full refund is available for any reason when requested within seven days of purchase.
  • After seven days, refunds are also provided for payment errors, duplicate charges or problems that prevent normal use of the feature.
  • Once refunded, the account's data sharing entitlement is removed. Records and local backups on your devices remain.

2. How to request a refund

codepretender.dev@gmail.com is the contact address. Send the email used for purchase and the order number from your receipt. You can also request a refund directly from the payment provider's receipt email. Refunds go to the original payment method and may take several days to appear, depending on the card provider.

3. Store purchases

For purchases through Microsoft Store, App Store or Google Play, follow that store's refund procedure. Data sharing access is also removed after a store refund is completed.

4. Statutory rights

This policy does not limit withdrawal rights under applicable laws, including Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc.
