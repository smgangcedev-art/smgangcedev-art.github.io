Support and user guide

Codebitda C++ · Java · AI

Codebitda is available as a public service. New registrations are currently closed. Visit each app's overview page for downloads.

Instructors and teaching assistants

Existing instructors can sign in with Google, connect Drive and manage class folders. Teaching assistants participate using accounts invited by an instructor. New instructor registrations are currently closed.

Students

Use the app and question file provided by your instructor. Students can participate in assignments and exams without Google login. Answers are submitted to the instructor's Drive.

Submission and recovery issues

Include the app name and version, the time of the error and the error text shown on screen. Do not overwrite or delete original backups; ask your instructor for recovery assistance. Do not publish recovery keys or passwords or attach them to support emails.

Account and privacy requests

Send service account deletion and data requests to the email address below. For retention, correction or deletion of answers in an instructor's Drive, also contact the instructor for that class.
